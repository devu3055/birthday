03 — A LETTER FOR YOU

For Heet, on your birthday

To my favorite person,

Happy Birthday to the boy who slowly became my best friend, my param mitra, my home, and now my love.

If someone had shown me our future when we first started talking on Instagram, I probably wouldn't have believed them. We went from strangers, to friends, to best friends, to something that neither of us could ignore.

I love all the little chapters we've collected — our first call, our first ride, our first meeting, the nervous little moments, the laughs, the hugs, the kisses, the gifts, the jatra, and finally the moment when our feelings became official.

Thank you for choosing me. Thank you for understanding me. Thank you for being the person I can laugh with, annoy, trust, and love all at once.

Today I only have one wish: that life gives you everything you dream of — and that, whenever you look beside you, I'm still there.

Happy birthday, my Heet.
From your Devanshi, with all my heart. ♡
